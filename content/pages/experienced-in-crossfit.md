## Welcome to CrossFit Warrington

### Transform Your Body at CrossFit Warrington

If you’ve already got experience in the gym, you don’t need to start from scratch. Whether you’re dropping in while you’re visiting the area or looking for a new long-term home for your training, we’ll make the transition simple, straightforward and genuinely welcoming.

There’s no long re-onboarding process or weeks of working your way back through the basics. We’ll just start with a quick movement assessment with one of our coaches. It gives us a chance to understand your experience, see how you move, talk through any current goals or limitations, and make sure you’re comfortable with the way we train.

Once that’s done, you’re ready to get involved with the main timetable and train alongside the rest of the community.

Come in, meet the coaches and members, check out the kit, and experience the atmosphere for yourself. Whether you’re chasing your next PB, building consistency, competing, or simply looking for a place where you enjoy training, you’ll find everything you need to get stuck straight in.
