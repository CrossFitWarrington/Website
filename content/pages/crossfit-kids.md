## Crossfit Kids and Juniors

CrossFit Warrington Kids is an exciting and dynamic programme designed to introduce children and teenagers to fitness in a fun, safe, and supportive environment. Tailored to suit the developmental needs of kids and teens of all fitness levels, our sessions focus on building strength, coordination, and flexibility through age-appropriate functional movements.

### Fun. Fitness. Confidence. For Life.

![A boy doing a push-up in a CrossFit Kids class](/assets/pages/crossfit-kids-1.jpg)

CrossFit Warrington Kids classes in Woolston are designed for children aged 4 and up, combining fun workouts with functional movements to build strength, coordination, and confidence. In a safe and supportive environment, kids develop lifelong healthy habits, improve social skills, and learn to enjoy fitness from an early age. Whether your child is new to exercise or already active, our program encourages growth through play and positive reinforcement.

### Who is it for?

![A girl running across the gym floor](/assets/pages/crossfit-kids-2.jpg)

CrossFit Warrington Kids classes are ideal for children aged 4 and above who want to stay active, healthy, and confident. Whether they’re just starting out or already love fitness, our program suits all ability levels. Sessions are run in 6-week blocks at our Woolston gym in small groups, allowing our certified coaches to give each child the attention and encouragement they need to thrive in a positive and structured environment.
