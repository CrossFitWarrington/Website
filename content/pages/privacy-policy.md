Effective Date: 02/05/2025

CrossFit Warrington Ltd ("we", "our", "us") is committed to safeguarding your personal data. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

### 1\. Information We Collect

-   Name, address, email, phone number
-   Emergency contact details
-   Health and injury information
-   Billing and payment data
-   Usage data from WodBoard or other software
-   Website interaction data (via cookies)

### 2\. How We Use Your Data

We use data to:

-   Register you as a member
-   Deliver coaching and support services
-   Process payments
-   Manage health and safety
-   Contact you with service updates or marketing (if opted in)

### 3\. Legal Basis

We process your data under UK GDPR based on:

-   Consent
-   Contractual necessity
-   Legal obligation
-   Legitimate interests

### 4\. Data Sharing

We may share data with:

-   WodBoard or payment processors
-   Emergency services
-   Legal authorities (if required)
-   We never sell your personal data.

### 5\. Your Rights

You may:

-   Access, correct, or delete your data
-   Restrict or object to processing
-   Withdraw consent
-   Lodge a complaint with the ICO

### 6\. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected. Financial data is kept for six years (HMRC compliance).

### 7\. Security

We use security measures to protect your data. Only authorised staff have access.

### 8\. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your web experience. You may accept or reject cookies when prompted.

## Fair Use Policy

To ensure fair access and service quality for all members:

-   Memberships are intended for individual, non-transferable use.
-   Abuse of booking privileges (e.g. repeated late cancellations or no-shows) may result in restricted access.
-   Respect for coaches, staff, equipment, and fellow members is expected at all times.
-   We reserve the right to revoke access if behaviour is deemed unsafe, disruptive, or in breach of this policy.

## Terms & Conditions

### 1\. Membership

-   Members must complete health screening and waiver forms before participation.
-   Monthly fees are paid in advance and are non-refundable (except under special agreement).
-   Late payments may suspend access.

### 2\. Bookings & Cancellations

-   Class spaces must be booked in advance via WodBoard.
-   A minimum 4 hours’ notice is required for cancellations.
