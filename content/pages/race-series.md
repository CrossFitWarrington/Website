## R.A.C.E

Our RACE (Resilience & Conditioning Event) series is designed to push your physical and mental boundaries through high-intensity, functional fitness challenges. We host events throughout the year to keep your training on track and your goals in sight.

![An athlete pushing a weighted sled along the turf](/assets/pages/race-series-1.jpg)

## The Seasonal Series

- Summer Series : Designed to replicate elite race conditions. You will face a series of stations that test your functional strength and cardiovascular capacity to the max.
- Winter Series: We maintain the same high intensity as the summer, but shift to a collaborative format. You and a partner will work through 13 challenging stations inside to cross the finish line together.

## Format

Choose the format that best matches your current goals:

- PRO: For the advanced athlete looking to test themselves against the highest standard.
- OPEN: Suitable for those who want the full experience at a competitive but accessible level.
- Doubles: Team up with a partner to conquer the course together.
- Solo: The ultimate test of individual resilience and conditioning.

## Details

- When: Various Sundays, keep an eye on Social Media.
- Who: Open to all—both current members and non-members are welcome.

## What to bring

Hydration: A bottle of water to keep you refreshed.
