## Welcome

Join us at CrossFit Warrington, where our adaptive CrossFit training celebrates the incredible resilience and strength of all our athletes. Our programmes are physio-led, ensuring that we safely support individuals living with a range of neurological conditions, from the recovery journey following a stroke to managing the symptoms of Parkinson’s.

![Four of the CrossFit Warrington adaptive team standing together](/assets/pages/adaptive-crossfit-1.jpg)

## Why Choose Our Adaptive Programme?

Our sessions in Woolston are more than just a workout; they are a path to greater independence and wellbeing. Whether you are focusing on post-stroke rehabilitation or managing a neurological condition, our expert team helps you improve:

- Mobility & Gait: Enhanced movement patterns, balance, and stability.
- Strength & Energy: Building functional capacity to support your daily life.
- Confidence: Boosting self-esteem through tangible, personal achievements.
- Long-term Health: Targeted support for managing symptoms and reducing future health risks.

## Your Experience, Our Priority

We have built our facility to remove barriers, featuring level access throughout and specialised equipment, including support bars, to ensure you can train safely and effectively. Every new member begins with a personal assessment to ensure their programme is perfectly tailored to their unique needs and goals.
