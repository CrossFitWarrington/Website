### Getting Started

For most people, the best first step is our 1-2-1 Foundations programme—a relaxed, supportive way to build confidence before joining group classes.

If you’ve completed our Couch to CrossFit course, you’re also very welcome to jump straight into memberships if you feel ready. And if you’d still like a bit more guidance, Foundations is always a great option.

### 1-2-1 Foundations — £99

Three 1-hour private sessions with a coach to help you feel comfortable and confident with the basics. Perfect if you’d like a gentler introduction or a bit of extra support before classes.

Includes one month of unlimited access once you've completed your three sessions.

## More about each option

### Drop-in and class pass

Perfect for those who may train at other gym, use a mix of training styles, or live away from the area. Drop-ins are for experienced CrossFitters visiting or training with us now and then.

## Additional Services - Personal Training

Whether you are new to CrossFit® or a long standing member, our Team offers 1-2-1 training to help you achieve new skills or develop those existing ones!

### Personal training - 1 hour

Enjoy personalised one-to-one coaching where every session is designed around your goals, with time, attention, and support focused solely on you.

Add this to your membership and save!

### Pack of 3 personal training sessions

Three one-hour sessions with a coach, built around your goals.

### Group training - 1 hour

Don’t just prepare to compete — prepare to perform at your best. From grassroots youth teams to elite-level athletes, our training supports every stage of development. Get in touch to discuss your team’s needs and discover how tailored strength and conditioning can enhance performance, resilience, and confidence.

## Specialist Services

### Individualised Nutrition Coaching

Support your progress in the gym by improving the habits that happen outside of it. This 12-week programme focuses on realistic, sustainable changes and includes an initial kick-off consultation, personalised calorie and macro guidance, weekly check-ins, ongoing support, and access to a private WhatsApp group for recipes, tips, and motivation.

### Online coaching

Whether you train at your local globo gym or in your home gym, our CrossFit Warrington online coaching programs are designed for you. Workouts are fully scalable, so you can follow world-class programming no matter where you are or what equipment you have.

### CrossFit Kids - 6 week block

Give your child the chance to move, play, and build confidence in a fun and encouraging setting. Suitable for ages 4 and up, our 6-week blocks help children develop strength, coordination, and healthy habits — without pressure, just progress.

### Couch to CrossFit - 6 week block

Our Couch to CrossFit programme is designed for anyone who’s curious but unsure where to start. Over six weeks, our qualified CrossFit coaches will guide you step by step, helping you learn the fundamentals in a friendly, supportive environment. You’ll build confidence with each movement, master proper technique safely, and progress at a pace that feels right for you. Whether you’ve never been to a gym or are returning after a break, Couch to CrossFit gives you the chance to start strong.
