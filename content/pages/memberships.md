### Couch to CrossFit - 6 week block — £50

Our Couch to CrossFit programme is designed for anyone who’s curious but unsure where to start. You’ll build confidence with each movement, master proper technique safely, and progress at a pace that feels right for you. Whether you’ve never been to a gym or are returning after a break, Couch to CrossFit gives you the chance to start strong.
