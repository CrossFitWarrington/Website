## Welcome!

### Book a trial

Looking to start your CrossFit journey but want to come along and try a session first. Our trial session gives you the chance to experience exactly how we do things here at CrossFit Warrington. Jump into a class, meet the team and take a look around our facility.

[Trial class](https://www.wodboard.com/locations/620/trials/bfa9cff5c7)

![Four CrossFit Warrington coaches smiling together](/assets/pages/new-to-crossfit-2.jpg)

### Speak to the team

To ensure you feel safe, confident, and ready for our regular classes, all newcomers must complete a foundations course before joining standard sessions.

Choose Your Option:

- Fast-Track 1-2-1: A flexible, personalised foundations course scheduled entirely around your days and times.
- Couch to CrossFit: A supportive monthly group course designed to build your skills alongside fellow beginners (Sunday's 9:00am)

Not Sure Which Option is Right for You?

If you have any questions or feel a little unsure about how to get involved, speak to one of our team members. We are happy to guide you toward the best choice for your goals and answer any questions along the way.

[Contact us](mailto:hello@crossfitwarrington.com?subject=More%20info)

![A coach talking a member through a movement](/assets/pages/new-to-crossfit-3.jpg)

### Book your foundations

Three 1-hour private sessions with a coach to help you feel comfortable and confident with the basics. Perfect if you’d like a gentler introduction or a bit of extra support before classes.

Includes a FREE one-month unlimited membership to get you started.

[Book here](https://www.wodboard.com/locations/620/signup/bfa9cff5c7?purchasable=Plan-8965)

### Couch to CrossFit course

Over six weeks, our qualified CrossFit coaches will guide you step by step, helping you learn the fundamentals in a friendly, supportive environment. You’ll build confidence with each movement, master proper technique safely, and progress at a pace that feels right for you.

[Book here](mailto:hello@crossfitwarrington.com?subject=Couch)
