This weekend, we had four of our members take on HYROX Manchester, and another member step up to complete a 10K run. Watching them all perform, push themselves, and cross those finish lines got me thinking about what truly makes someone “fit”.

At CrossFit, we often talk about fitness in a very specific way.

CrossFit defines fitness as work capacity across broad time and modal domains.

In simple terms, it’s about how much work you can get done.

That work might be:

- Running
- Lifting
- Rowing
- Gymnastics
- Or a combination of all of them

The time domain refers to how long you’re working for. Sometimes that’s short and intense—three minutes like _Fran_. Other times it’s longer efforts such as a 10-minute workout, a 20-minute EMOM, _Murph_, or even sessions lasting an hour or more.

The modal domains are the different types of movement we train:

- Weightlifting
- Powerlifting
- Running
- Skipping
- Jumping
- Rowing
- Gymnastics
- And much more

Put simply, CrossFit doesn’t just train you to be good at one thing. It trains you to be good at many things, across many time frames.

### Preparing You for the Unknown

One of the core aims of CrossFit is to prepare you for the unknown and the unknowable.

Life doesn’t hand you challenges in neat little packages. One day it might be carrying shopping up the stairs. Another day it might be chasing after your kids. Another day it might be signing up for a HYROX, a 10K, or a charity event you never thought you’d do.

CrossFit builds a broad, well-rounded level of fitness that transfers to almost any physical challenge. That’s exactly what we saw this weekend—members stepping confidently into events outside the gym and performing brilliantly.

### When Specific Goals Matter

Now, it’s important to say this: if you have a specific goal, specificity matters.

- Training for a triathlon? You’ll need more swimming, cycling, and running.
- Training for HYROX? You’ll need significantly more running and race-specific conditioning.
- Training for a marathon? Your running volume will increase.

These are targeted adaptations for targeted outcomes.

And that’s absolutely fine.

In fact, it’s encouraged.

### The Power of a CrossFit Foundation

What CrossFit gives you is the foundation.

It builds:

- Strength
- Endurance
- Power
- Mobility
- Mental resilience
- Confidence

From that base, you can layer in more specific training to suit your goals—without losing your overall fitness.

That’s why our members can go from regular CrossFit classes to tackling HYROX, 10Ks, obstacle races, hiking challenges, or charity events with confidence. They’re not starting from scratch. They’re building on years of well-rounded training.

### More Than Just Workouts

For me, this weekend wasn’t just about times, rankings, or medals.

It was about seeing people believe in themselves.

Seeing people say:  
“I never thought I’d do something like that… but I did.”

That’s what real fitness is about. It’s not just what happens inside the gym. It’s how capable, confident, and resilient you feel outside of it.
