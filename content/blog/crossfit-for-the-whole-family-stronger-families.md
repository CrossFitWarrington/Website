At CrossFit Warrington, based in Woolston, Cheshire, we believe fitness should fit around real life — and real families. That’s why we’re proud to offer our CrossFit Family Programme, welcoming parents with newborns right through to school-age children, every Monday at 8:00am.

You don’t need any previous gym or CrossFit experience to get started. Our sessions are supportive, inclusive, and designed to help parents move, train, and connect — all while their children are right there with them.

A Family-Friendly CrossFit Experience

Our family sessions allow parents to train in a relaxed, welcoming environment where babies can stay close by and younger children can safely take part or play alongside. Everything is fully scalable, and the emphasis is on confidence, consistency, and community, not perfection.

Lead Coach Ben Ecob, who trains alongside his own family at CrossFit Warrington, shares his perspective:

“As a coach — and a parent — it means a lot to be able to train in a space that genuinely supports family life. My wife and child attend our family sessions, and it shows what CrossFit Warrington is really about: helping families stay healthy together without pressure or judgement.”

— Ben Ecob, Lead Coach, CrossFit Warrington

Parents benefit not just physically — gaining strength, energy, and improved wellbeing — but also mentally and socially. Training with other local parents in Woolston creates connection, routine, and a much-needed support network.

CrossFit Kids & Juniors in Warrington

Alongside our family programme, CrossFit Warrington also runs dedicated CrossFit Kids and Juniors classes every Sunday at 8:00am, delivered in a 6-week block with two sessions per week.

These classes are carefully structured to be:

- Fun and engaging
- Age-appropriate
- Safe and fully coached
- Confidence-building

Children learn fundamental movement skills such as running, jumping, throwing, lifting, and balancing — all taught through games, teamwork, and positive reinforcement.

Kids Coach Sally explains how the younger sessions run:

“Our kids classes are all about fun and movement. We use games to teach strong foundations — coordination, balance, and listening skills — without the kids ever feeling like they’re in a ‘lesson’.”

— Sally, CrossFit Kids Coach

For older children, the Juniors programme introduces more structure while keeping sessions encouraging and enjoyable.

“In our juniors classes, we focus on technique, confidence, and effort. We want every child to leave feeling proud of what they’ve achieved and excited to come back.”

— Jess, CrossFit Juniors Coach

Why Children’s Fitness Matters

Across the UK, schools and the national curriculum increasingly highlight the importance of physical literacy, regular activity, and developing movement skills from a young age. Fitness plays a key role in supporting:

- Concentration and learning
- Mental health and emotional wellbeing
- Confidence and self-esteem
- Long-term healthy habits

Our CrossFit Kids and Juniors programmes align closely with these goals, focusing on broad, well-rounded physical development rather than early specialisation.

Owner Sian Edwards explains the vision behind the programmes at CrossFit Warrington:

“We’re passionate about building confident, capable children who enjoy being active. When kids see their parents training too, it reinforces positive habits and shows that health and fitness are a normal part of everyday life.”

— Sian Edwards, Owner, CrossFit Warrington
