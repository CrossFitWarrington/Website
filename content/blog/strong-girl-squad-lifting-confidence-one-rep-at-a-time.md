This term, **Year 8 girls from Beaumont Academy** in Warrington are taking part in something powerful: the **Strong Girl Squad (SGS)**—a unique program created by **Sian Edwards**, owner of CrossFit® Warrington, to help girls aged 11–16 discover their strength through weightlifting, movement, and mindset.

### Why Strong Girl Squad?

We know that many girls lose interest in sport during their teenage years—often due to confidence issues, lack of support, or the pressure to fit a certain image. **Strong Girl Squad is here to change that.**

This isn’t just about lifting weights—it's about lifting _each other_, building confidence, and learning that strength is something to be proud of.

### What the Program Includes

**💪 Girls-Only Weightlifting Workshops**  
Our six-week structured program introduces girls to the fundamentals of weightlifting in a safe, fun, and supportive space. It’s tailored for beginners and focused on technique, progression, and body awareness—showing that lifting is for _every body_.

**🧠 Confidence & Well-Being**  
We believe strength goes beyond the gym floor. Each session incorporates discussions on:

- Self-esteem
- Positive body image
- Healthy habits
- Nutrition and recovery

All designed to help girls build resilience and feel empowered in and out of sport.

**💜 Breaking Barriers**  
No kit? No problem. We supply sportswear, toiletries, and other essentials so that _no one is left out_ due to financial or material barriers. Every girl deserves a chance to thrive.

### The Impact

Since launching, the Strong Girl Squad has already made a huge impact:

- Girls are learning that being strong is something to celebrate
- Participants are showing up with more confidence and self-belief
- Friendships are forming through shared challenge and support

This is more than just a fitness program—it’s a movement of young women redefining what it means to be strong.
