Think you're not a "racer"? Most of the people who line up at our R.A.C.E. events didn't think they were either.

R.A.C.E. is our indoor functional fitness race series at CrossFit Warrington. It's a chance to test yourself against the clock, in a room full of people who are all doing the same thing: giving it their best. The next one, **R.A.C.E. Winter, is on Sunday 20 December, with heats from 9am.**

## What is R.A.C.E.?

A race run indoors at our gym in Kingsland Grange, built around functional fitness. You race in heats, so you're always side by side with other people, with a crowd cheering you on, rather than grinding away on your own in a corner.

## Who is it for?

Everyone. Members, friends, family and first-timers all line up together. Every race is scaled to you, so a beginner and a seasoned athlete can both get a proper, hard, fun race out of the same event. You don't need to be "CrossFit fit" to take part. You just need to turn up.

## Why bother in the middle of winter?

Dark mornings and cold nights are when most people's training drifts. A date in the diary changes that. Knowing you have a race on 20 December gives you a reason to show up in November, and a great way to finish the year feeling proud of what you did.

## How to get ready

Nothing fancy: keep coming to class, train consistently, and ask your coach to help you decide how to scale your race. If you're brand new, start with a [free trial class](https://www.wodboard.com/locations/620/trials/bfa9cff5c7) or our [Couch to CrossFit course](https://crossfitwarrington.com/couch-to-crossfit), and you'll be ready sooner than you think. There are five weeks to go, which is plenty of time.

## How to enter

Message us on Instagram or Facebook with the word **RACE**, or ask at the gym, and we'll get you entered.
