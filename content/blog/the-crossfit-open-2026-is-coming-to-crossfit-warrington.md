It’s almost that time again!

The CrossFit Open 2026 is just around the corner, and here at CrossFit Warrington we’re getting ready for one of our favourite times of the year.

Over three weeks, CrossFit gyms all over the world will complete the same three workouts. While the Open is global, the magic really happens locally — on the gym floor, surrounded by your coaches, training partners, and friends.

The Open is about:

- Showing up
- Supporting one another
- Celebrating progress
- Being part of something bigger than a normal class

### Why 2026 Is Extra Special

2026 marks **20 years of the journey to finding the Fittest on Earth**, and the **20th CrossFit Games** — a huge milestone in CrossFit history.

Whether you love the competitive side or simply enjoy the atmosphere, this is a brilliant opportunity to get involved.

### The CrossFit Games & The Open: A Quick Overview

The CrossFit Games is a global competition with divisions for:

- Individuals
- Teams
- Age groups
- Adaptive athletes

The season begins with **The CrossFit Open** — a three-week, open-to-everyone event where one workout is released each week.

For a small number of athletes, the Open is the first step on the competitive pathway.  
For most people, it’s something very different.

The Open is:

- A chance to test yourself
- A chance to train alongside your gym mates
- A chance to try things you might not normally attempt in class
- A chance to feel part of the wider CrossFit community

At CrossFit Warrington, the Open isn’t about leaderboards. It’s about participation, encouragement, and shared experiences.

### Key Dates – CrossFit Open 2026

Pop these dates in your diary:

**Open Registration Opens:**  
- January 14th

**The CrossFit Open:**  
- February 26th – March 16th

**Workout Windows:**  
- Open 26.1: February 26th – March 2nd
- Open 26.2: March 5th – March 9th
- Open 26.3: March 12th – March 16th

Each workout is released on a Thursday, giving you a long weekend to complete it and (if registered) submit your score.

### First Step: Register for the Open

There are two ways to take part at CrossFit Warrington:

1. Register officially for the CrossFit Open online
2. Book your workout slot at CrossFit Warrington each week

You **don’t have to register** to join in with the workouts at the gym — but we strongly encourage it.

**Why register?**

- It supports the Open as a global event
- It connects our Warrington community with thousands of gyms worldwide
- It gives you a personal record of your progress

You can’t add scores later, so even setting your first benchmark is a big deal — future you will be glad you did.

Most importantly, registering makes the Open feel special. It stops being “just another workout” and becomes a shared annual milestone we experience together as a gym.

### Why the CrossFit Open Feels Different

There’s something about the Open that changes the atmosphere in the gym.

People:

- Try things they’ve been avoiding
- Surprise themselves
- Get first pull-ups, first toes-to-bar, or simply keep moving when they didn’t think they could

And they do it while the whole room is cheering, counting reps, and encouraging them to keep going.

At CrossFit Warrington, this is what the Open is really about: **support, confidence, and community**.

### What’s Happening at CrossFit Warrington During the Open?

Throughout the Open, we’ll be running **special Open workout sessions** where members can:

- Complete the workout together
- Judge one another
- Soak up the atmosphere

These sessions will be:

- Welcoming and inclusive
- Suitable for all abilities
- Focused on fun, effort, and encouragement

You’re also welcome to hang around, cheer people on, and be part of the buzz — even if you’re not working out that day.

Full details on booking and session times will be shared closer to the start date.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How fit do I need to be?**  
You don’t need to be “fit enough”. Every workout has RX, Scaled, and Foundations options, plus age group and adaptive divisions.

**Can beginners take part?**  
Absolutely. If you attend group classes at CrossFit Warrington, you’re ready. Many people do their first Open with only a few months of training.

**Do I have to register?**  
No — but we strongly recommend it for the full Open experience.

**What if I can’t make the main sessions?**  
No problem. As long as you complete the workout within the submission window, you’re good to go.

**What if I’m away one of the weeks?**  
Affiliates worldwide all do the same workouts. You can drop into another gym and still submit your score.

### Not a Member of CrossFit Warrington Yet?

If you’re not currently a member but would love to be involved in the Open, get in touch. Whether you’re CrossFit-curious, visiting the area, or looking for a new gym community, we’re happy to chat through your options.

Get the dates in the diary, keep an eye on updates, and get ready.

**The CrossFit Open 2026 is coming — and we can’t wait to experience it together at CrossFit Warrington.**
