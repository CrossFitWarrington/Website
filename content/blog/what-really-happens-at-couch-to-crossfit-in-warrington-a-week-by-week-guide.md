Walking into a gym for the first time is the hardest part. You don't know the movements, you don't know anyone, and you're worried everyone else will be fitter than you.

That's exactly why we built **Couch to CrossFit**: a small group course at CrossFit Warrington made for complete beginners. No experience needed, no fitness level required, and a coach with you for every rep.

Here's what actually happens.

## Your first class is free

Every Couch to CrossFit block starts with a free taster class. You come along, meet the coaches and the group, and try a session with no commitment. If you enjoy it (most people do), you carry on for the next five weeks.

**What to bring:** comfy clothes you can move in, trainers, a water bottle. That's it. Parking is on the main road right in front of the building.

## Weeks 1–2: learning the basics

The first couple of weeks are all about **how to move well**. We teach the foundations step by step:

- squatting (to a box to start with, if that suits you)
- hinging and lifting safely from the floor
- pushing and pulling
- simple rowing and bike work

Nothing is rushed. Every movement is scaled to you: lighter weights, a higher box, fewer reps, whatever you need on the day.

## Weeks 3–4: putting it together

Once the movements feel familiar, we start linking them into short, simple workouts. This is where people usually surprise themselves. The workouts are short, everyone finishes together, and the group cheers each other on.

You'll start to notice small wins: getting up off the floor more easily, carrying the shopping without thinking about it, feeling less out of breath on the stairs.

## Weeks 5–6: building confidence

By the end of the block you'll know the key movements, understand how a class works, and feel at home in the gym. Most importantly, you'll know the coaches and the people training alongside you.

From there, many of our Couch to CrossFit members move into regular classes or a Foundations membership, or simply join the next block to keep building.

## Who is Couch to CrossFit for?

- People who have **never set foot in a gym**
- People coming back after a long break, an injury or a busy few years
- Anyone who's thought "CrossFit looks great, but I'm not fit enough yet"

If that's you, this is the course for you. You don't get fit to start. You start to get fit.

## Why a small group?

Big classes can feel intimidating when you're new. Couch to CrossFit keeps numbers small so the coaches can watch every movement, give you feedback and make sure you're working at the right level, never pushed beyond it and never judged.

## How to join

Couch to CrossFit runs on **Sundays at 9am** at CrossFit Warrington, 14 Greys Court, Kingsland Grange, WA1 4SH. Blocks run for six weeks (a free taster class plus five weeks), and the next block starts in the new year.

**Want a spot?** Send us a message with the word **"COUCH"** on Instagram or Facebook, or get in touch through the website, and we'll save you a place in the free first class.

Your first step is the hardest one. We'll help with the rest.
