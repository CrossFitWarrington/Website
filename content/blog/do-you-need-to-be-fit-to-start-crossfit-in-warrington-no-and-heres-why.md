It's the most common thing we hear at CrossFit Warrington: *"I'll join once I'm a bit fitter."*

We get it. CrossFit has a reputation for heavy barbells, burpees and people collapsing on the floor. The truth is, most of our members walked through the door feeling exactly the same way.

## We scale workouts. We don't change them.

Everyone in the class does the same workout, but your version is built around you. If the workout calls for a 40 kg barbell, you might use 15 kg or a pair of dumbbells. If it says pull-ups, you might do ring rows. If it's 100 reps, you might do 60.

The workout gives everyone the same feeling, whatever your starting point. That's why a 25-year-old rugby player and someone who hasn't trained in 20 years can share the same class and both get a lot out of it.

## The three ways we scale

- **Lighter loads:** we pick a weight that lets you move well, not one that makes you grind.
- **Easier variations:** every movement has a version you can do today.
- **Fewer reps or shorter time:** we finish when it's right for you, not when the whiteboard says.

Your coach sorts all of this with you before the clock starts, so you never have to guess.
