When people hear the word “CrossFit,” they often picture elite athletes lifting heavy weights or competing at a high level. At CrossFit Warrington, we’re changing that perception every single day.

We believe fitness should be accessible, inclusive, and empowering for everyone—regardless of age, experience, ability, or background. Whether you’re a teacher, a CEO, a busy parent, a teenager discovering fitness for the first time, or a grandmother wanting to stay strong and independent, you’ll find your place here.

At CrossFit Warrington, our community is built around one simple promise:

We have someone just like you.

More Than a Gym – A Fitness Family

Located in Woolston, Warrington, CrossFit Warrington has grown into much more than a place to exercise. It’s a welcoming community where members support each other, celebrate victories together, and encourage one another through challenges.

Owner and Head Coach Sian Edwards has built an environment where nobody feels left behind.

“We believe that exercise and fitness should fit the person, not the other way around,” says Sian Edwards.

“Every movement can be adapted, every workout can be scaled, and every person deserves to feel capale, confident, and supported.”

That philosophy is at the heart of everything we do.

CrossFit for Kids and Juniors

Building healthy habits starts early.

Our Kids and Juniors programmes help young people develop confidence, coordination, strength, and teamwork in a fun and safe environment. Instead of focusing on appearance or performance, we focus on helping children enjoy movement and discover what their bodies can do.

Whether they’re sporty, shy, energetic, or completely new to exercise, every child is encouraged to progress at their own pace while being part of a supportive group.

Adaptive Fitness: Movement Without Limits

One of the areas we’re most proud of is our Adaptive Fitness programme.

Designed for individuals living with long-term health conditions, injuries, disabilities, or mobility challenges, these sessions demonstrate what makes CrossFit truly unique: everything can be adapted.

From seated workouts to tailored movement plans, our coaches work closely with each participant to ensure they feel safe, supported, and successful.

“CrossFit isn’t about fitting into a mould,” says Sian. “It’s about finding what works for you and helping you move better, feel stronger, and live with greater confidence.”

Recent adaptive sessions have welcomed people living with Parkinson’s and other conditions, proving that fitness can be life-changing at any stage of life.

From Teachers to CEOs

Walk into any class at CrossFit Warrington and you’ll find people from every walk of life.

Teachers training before school.

Business owners squeezing in a lunchtime session.

Healthcare professionals unwinding after a long shift.

Parents reclaiming time for themselves.

Retirees building strength and independence.

The beauty of CrossFit is that everyone completes the same workout together—but each person does it at a level that suits them.

No one is expected to keep up with anyone else.

The only competition is becoming a better version of yourself.

Grandmothers, Grandfathers, and Everyone In Between

Some of our most inspiring members are proving that age is just a number.

We’ve seen members in their sixties, seventies, and beyond improve their strength, mobility, balance, and confidence through consistent training.

Functional fitness helps people perform everyday tasks more easily—whether that’s carrying shopping bags, climbing stairs, playing with grandchildren, or simply maintaining independence for longer.

“The most rewarding thing isn’t seeing someone lift a heavier weight,” says Sian. “It’s seeing someone do something they never thought they’d be able to do again.”

Why People Stay

People often join CrossFit Warrington to improve their fitness.

They stay because of the community.

Members quickly discover that they’re surrounded by people cheering them on, celebrating every achievement, and supporting them through setbacks.

There are no cliques. No intimidation. No pressure to be perfect.

Just real people helping each other become stronger, healthier, and happier.

Your Journey Starts Here

If you’ve ever thought:

- “I’m not fit enough.”
- “I’m too old.”
- “I’ve never exercised before.”
- “I wouldn’t fit in.”

Then CrossFit Warrington was built for you.

Whether you’re 4 or 80, an athlete or a complete beginner, living with a health condition or simply looking for a new challenge, there’s a place for you here.

Because at CrossFit Warrington, we’re more than a gym.

We’re a fitness family.

And we promise — we have someone just like you.
