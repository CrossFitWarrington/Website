If you’ve ever looked at joining CrossFit Warrington, you might have asked us this exact question.  
The short answer is no, we don't have a beginner's class. Before you click away thinking you need to be a seasoned athlete to walk through our doors, hear us out. There is a very intentional method behind how we do things, and it ultimately makes you fitter, faster, and more confident than any segregated "beginner's hour" ever could.

### Why We Don’t Do "Beginner Only" Classes

At CrossFit Warrington, we don't believe in sticking beginners in a corner on their own for months on end. Here is why:

- Exceptional Coaching: Our coaches are experts at scaling and adapting workouts on the fly. Whether it is your very first day holding a barbell or you are a CrossFit Games quarterfinalist doing advanced gymnastics, everyone is in the same room doing the same workout.
- Meeting the Stimulus: CrossFit is infinitely scalable. Our coaches ensure that every single person—regardless of current fitness level—receives the exact same physiological stimulus safely and effectively.
- Inclusivity and Adaptability: We pride ourselves on thinking outside of the box to make fitness accessible to everyone. Our coaches regularly adapt sessions for people in wheelchairs, stroke survivors, and individuals managing various injuries or long-term health conditions. If there's a will, we will find a way to get you moving.
- Community and Culture: There is magic in our classes. When newcomers sweat side-by-side with experienced athletes, the energy is electric. You aren’t isolated; you are welcomed, supported, and motivated by people who were in your exact shoes not long ago.

> _"We don't believe in putting people in boxes or keeping them on the sidelines in a permanent beginner's class,"_ says gym owner Sian Edwards. _"Our goal from day one is to integrate you into the full community. Because our coaches are exceptional at scaling, you get the exact right workout for your body—whether it's your first time touching a barbell or your thousandth."_

### How You Get Started: The Foundations

While we don't keep you locked in a perpetual beginner's class, we do take your safety and education seriously. Before jumping straight into our regular group classes, you need to learn the fundamental movements.  
You have two ways to build your base with us:

1. Couch to CrossFit: Our dedicated beginner's course designed to ease you into the methodology at a comfortable pace.
2. Foundations 1-to-1 Sessions: Three private sessions tailored specifically to you, your goals, and your movement patterns.

### What Happens After That?

Once you finish your Couch to CrossFit course or your three 1-to-1 Foundations sessions, you are fully equipped to join our standard classes.  
Do you have to stay on a beginner's track after that? No, absolutely not. You dive right into the full community experience, trusting that your coaches have your back, scale every movement to your needs, and help you grow every single time you step onto the gym floor.
