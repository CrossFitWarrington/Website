If you’ve ever wandered into a CrossFit box, you might have felt a flutter of nerves. It’s easy to look at the rig, the barbells, and the athletes launching themselves onto high wooden platforms and think, _‘Is this really for me?’_

At **CrossFit Warrington**, we want to set the record straight: CrossFit isn’t about where you start—it’s about where you’re going. And if you’ve seen our latest reel, you’ll know that the humble **Box Jump** is the perfect example of our philosophy: **everything is scalable, and everything is designed to grow with you.**

## Why Box Jumps? More Than Just Jumping

Box jumps are a staple in our training because they are brilliant for building explosive power, coordination, and functional strength. They’re a fantastic way to get your heart rate up and build cardiovascular stamina.

But let’s be honest: leaping onto a 30-inch box isn’t the starting point for everyone. Whether you are recovering from an injury, working with specific physical requirements, or are a young athlete just finding your feet, we have a way to make the movement work for you.

## Scaling for Everyone: No Excuses

Scaling is the 'secret sauce' that makes CrossFit accessible to absolutely everyone. We don’t change the _goal_ of the workout; we simply tailor the mechanics to match your current ability.

**Our experience speaks for itself:**

**Adaptive Athletes:** We have extensive experience working with adaptive athletes, modifying movements to ensure that the stimulus of the workout remains the same, regardless of physical challenges. If a jump isn't appropriate, we adapt the movement to ensure you still get the same workout intensity and benefits.

**Our Younger Members:** We love seeing our kids' classes tackle the same movements as the adults. By scaling the boxes and the volume, we teach children safe, foundational movement patterns that set them up for a lifetime of health.

Think of it as a progression ladder. Your journey might start with step-ups onto a small plate or a low box. This is all about mastering the movement and building confidence. As you get more comfortable, we add height, inch by inch, until you’re confidently jumping onto the 20”, 24”, or even 30” box. **There are truly no excuses—if you can move, we can scale it.**

## Coaching That Has Your Back

You aren’t doing this on your own. At CrossFit Warrington, our coaches are the heart of the community. They aren’t just there to keep an eye on the clock; they’re there to observe your movement patterns, offer tailored adjustments, and ensure you stay safe while you push your boundaries. They’ll know exactly when you’re ready to progress, and they’ll be the first to cheer when you nail it.

## A Note from Owner, Sian Edwards

We had a quick chat with our owner, **Sian Edwards**, about why this inclusive approach is so vital to what we do:

> _"At CrossFit Warrington, we don't believe in a 'one size fits all' approach. Whether you are an adaptive athlete, one of our younger members, or just starting out on a low plate, you are doing the work. We’ll provide the box, the coaching, and the community. You just need to bring the willingness to have a go.
