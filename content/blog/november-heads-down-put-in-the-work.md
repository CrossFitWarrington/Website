There's a moment in every workout when your head starts talking. *Slow down. Drop the weight. Nobody will notice if you skip a rep.* That voice is the real opponent, and this month we're training it.

November's focus at CrossFit Warrington is **mindset**.

## Stay on the task in front of you

The whiteboard can look scary: 3 rounds, 400 m runs, a long list of reps. Don't try to swallow the whole workout at once. Break it down. This round. This set. This rep. When you only have to win the next 30 seconds, you can keep going a lot longer than you think.

## Show up when you don't feel like it

Motivation comes and goes. Habits stick. The members who make the biggest changes aren't the ones who feel fired up every day. They're the ones who book the class, turn up, and trust the process. That's exactly why we're running the November challenge: hit 13 classes this month and you're in the draw for a free PT session.

## Be kind to yourself

A strong mindset isn't about beating yourself up. It's about talking to yourself like you'd talk to the person next to you: "You've got this. One more." Scale when you need to. Rest when you need to. Then go again.

## You're not doing it alone

That's the best bit about training here. When your head says stop, there's a coach and a room full of people who'll get you through the next rep.

So this month: heads down, put in the work, and see what you're capable of.
