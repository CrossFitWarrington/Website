At CrossFit Warrington, we’re committed to helping every member achieve meaningful, sustainable progress. Joining the gym is the first step, but continued success comes from setting clear goals, reviewing them regularly, and adapting as your journey evolves.

That’s why, after being part of our community for three months, you’ll be invited to book a Goal Review with one of our coaches.

> _“A goal properly set is halfway reached.”_  
> — Zig Ziglar

#### Why we do Goal Reviews

Progress doesn’t happen by accident — it happens through focus, consistency, and accountability. The three-month mark is an ideal time to pause and reflect on how far you’ve come, what’s working well, and where you’d like to go next.

During your Goal Review, we will:

- Assess your progress – looking at improvements in fitness, confidence, and consistency.
- Revisit your goals – ensuring they’re still relevant to your current needs and lifestyle.
- Identify new targets – whether performance-based, aesthetic, or wellbeing-focused.
- Provide tailored support – from training strategies to nutritional guidance and ongoing accountability.

#### How it works

Once your review is booked, you’ll meet with a coach who will guide you through the process. Together, you’ll discuss your achievements, outline your next set of goals, and create a clear plan to support your continued development.

Our aim is to ensure that every member feels supported, challenged, and motivated — with a plan that evolves as you do.

#### A message from Sian, Owner of CrossFit Warrington

> _“At CFW, our goal reviews aren’t just about ticking boxes. They’re about connecting with our members, understanding what truly drives them, and making sure they have the support to keep moving forward. It’s about progress in every sense — in strength, mindset, and confidence.”_  
> — Sian, Owner & Head Coach, CrossFit Warrington

#### Your next step

When you receive your three-month invitation, simply book your Goal Review via Wodboard. It’s your opportunity to reflect, refocus, and keep building towards the results that matter most to you.

At CFW, we don’t just help you set goals — we help you achieve them.
