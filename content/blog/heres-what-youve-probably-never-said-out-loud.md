The nerves gripping your stomach as you sit in the car outside. The quick wipe of a tear before turning off the ignition. The quiet, persistent voice in the back of your mind whispering that you’re just not "the type" for a place like this.  
Let’s get one thing straight right now: None of that has anything to do with whether you belong here. It’s just what the fitness industry, social media, and years of self-doubt have taught you to believe.  
We’ve all been sold the myth that you need a "before" transformation story before you're allowed to step foot in a gym, or that you have to already be fit, intimidatingly confident, and wearing matching gear to earn your spot on the floor.

### The Truth About Walking Through Our Doors

If you look around the room at CrossFit Warrington, you won’t see a room full of elite athletes who woke up immune to self-doubt.  
You’ll see parents who snuck away from a chaotic morning, shift workers trying to reclaim their health, absolute beginners who had never touched a barbell, and people who sat in that exact same car crying or shaking before their very first session.

> "Fitness is just the start—we focus on building confidence, resilience, and lasting change. You don’t have to already feel ready. You just have to show up."  
> — Sian Edwards, Owner & Coach

### Why We Start With Foundations

That is exactly why we don’t just throw you into the deep end of a group class.  
Before joining our standard classes, new members go through our dedicated foundations program—including our 3 x 1-to-1 sessions designed specifically to help build your confidence from the ground up. This step is so important to us because your first days shouldn't be about keeping up with a crowd; they should be about you, a coach, and learning the basics at a pace that feels right for you.  
In a private setting, we break down the movements, look at your unique range of motion, and answer every question you have without any eyes on you. By the time you step onto the main gym floor, you won't be guessing—you'll _know_ you belong there.  
Every single movement we do—from the most complex barbell lift to a bodyweight squat—is infinitely scalable. Our coaching team is here to meet you exactly where you are today, not where you think you "should" be.

### Break the Cycle

The hardest part of your fitness journey isn't the workout. It’s turning the key in the ignition, opening the door, and stepping inside when every instinct tells you to drive away.  
You don't need to feel fearless. You just need to take that first step. We’ll take care of the rest.
