# Changing your website

You can change the words, prices, photos, coaches, FAQs and blog posts yourself. The layout and colours stay as they are, so nothing you type can break the look of the site.

## Getting in

1. Go to **crossfitwarrington.com/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub** and sign in as CrossFitWarrington (the same login you made on 24 September).

On the left you'll see three things:

- **Blog posts**: write a new post, or change an old one
- **Pages**: every page on the site, such as Memberships, Kids or Couch to CrossFit
- **Homepage and site details**: the homepage, your coaches, the FAQs, and your phone numbers and booking links

## Changing something

1. Click what you want to change, for example **Pages → From First Steps to Forever Fit** (the memberships page).
2. Change the words in the boxes.
3. Click **Publish** at the top, then **Publish now**.
4. Wait **about two minutes**, then refresh the website to see your change.

## Handy things to know

- **Bold**: put two stars either side, like `**this**`.
- **A new paragraph**: leave an empty line between them.
- **A blue button**: put a link on its own line. In the Pages and Blog boxes, use the link button in the toolbar.
- **Photos**: click the photo box, then **Upload** to use one from your phone or computer. Always fill in "Describe the photo" too. It helps blind visitors and Google.
- **A new coach**: Homepage and site details → Coaches → **Add coach**. Drag coaches up or down to change the order. The order is the same everywhere on the site.
- **A new blog post**: Blog posts → **New Blog post**. Fill in the title, date and a short summary, then write the post.
- **The timetable** comes straight from WodBoard. Change a class in WodBoard and the website updates by itself.

## If something goes wrong

- If a change doesn't appear after five minutes, the site has spotted a problem and kept the old version live, so visitors never see a broken page. Common causes are an empty box that has to be filled in, or the old 07764 number. Fix it and publish again, or message Trident.
- Every change is saved in the site's history, so anything can be put back. Just ask.

**Trident:** WhatsApp or email Ash any time.
